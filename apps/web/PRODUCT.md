# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Swiss SME owners: trades, shops, practices and small companies that need a website and are comparing studios. Often on a phone, rarely technical. They judge by whether the site feels trustworthy, is quick to understand and makes getting in touch easy.

## Product Purpose

The marketing site for Helvetic Studio, a Swiss web design and web engineering studio. It shows the work, explains the services and turns visitors into project enquiries. Success means an owner lands, understands what the studio does within seconds, trusts the craft and sends an enquiry.

## Brand Personality

Bold, sharp and modern, held in check by Swiss precision: calm, confident and exact. One saturated red, one typeface, a signature mountain range (the Churfirsten) that doubles as navigation. The quality shows in the details rather than in noise.

## Anti-references

- Generic agency and SaaS templates: gradient blobs, stock hero photos, identical icon-card grids.
- Loud, jittery motion; bounce and elastic easing.
- Anything that reads as a theme bought off a marketplace.

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what we preach.** The site is the portfolio: every state, breakpoint and transition must be correct, because clients read bugs as a lack of craft.
2. **The range is the navigation.** Summits are pages; motion between them is a camera move, never decoration.
3. **Clarity first for non-technical owners.** Plain language, obvious next steps, a contact route that never takes more than a click.
4. **Restraint with a sharp edge.** One red, one face, confident type; calm surfaces and precise detail do the work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Full keyboard navigation with visible focus, every animation has a reduced-motion alternative, and touch targets are at least 44px on mobile.
